## Want to learn sales directly from a founder while helping professors teach better?

Prova is an early-stage edtech startup helping university professors and higher-ed departments improve teaching and assessment. We’re hiring **one** Sales Development Representative to book demos, close early accounts, and grow with a small, founder-led team in Lehi.

### Why this role

- **Learn sales from the founder** — weekly 1:1 call reviews with Kaden, not a manager three levels up.
- **Your work shapes the company** — every client you bring in matters at this stage.
- **Help professors teach better** — mission-driven selling for people who care about education.

### Let’s be real about it

This role involves a lot of outreach, plenty of “no”s, and pay that’s partly commission. It’s **on-site in Lehi, Utah**. Relocation is required; we do **not** offer relocation assistance. We say this up front so you can decide if the mentorship and mission are worth the grind.

### What you’ll do

- Prospect and qualify faculty and departments (~35%)
- Book and run demos (~25%, ~8/month)
- Close contracts (~20%)
- First-semester client check-ins (~10%)
- CRM / quota hygiene (~5%)
- Weekly founder coaching (~5%)

**Success looks like:** 24+ demos and 5+ new accounts per quarter, with strong client satisfaction.

### Who thrives here

- **New grads:** Your first sales job, with real mentorship and a fast path to more responsibility.
- **Former educators:** Use what you know about teaching to help other educators.
- **Experienced reps:** More ownership, direct founder access, and bigger impact than at a big company.

0–2 years client-facing preferred. Bachelor’s preferred, not required. Target start ~May for the August academic ramp.

### Hiring process (preview)

1. Resume screen  
2. 15-min phone screen  
3. Written cold-email exercise  
4. Mock product demo + coaching  
5. Structured interview  
6. References + background check  
7. Offer  

About 10–11 weeks from posting to offer. Out-of-state candidates can complete later rounds same-day / by video.
