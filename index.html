  2. 02

The role

Sales Development Representative

Prospect and qualify faculty, book and run demos, close early contracts, and stay close to new clients— coached weekly by the founder in a small, high-collaboration team.

How you’ll spend your time

  • ~35% Prospect & qualify faculty and departments
  • ~25% Book and run demos (~8 / month)
  • ~20% Close contracts
  • ~10% First-semester client check-ins
  • ~5% CRM & quota hygiene
  • ~5% Weekly founder coaching

What success looks like

  • 24+ demos booked per quarter
  • 5+ new accounts closed per quarter
  • ~25% demo-to-close conversion target
  • 4.5 client satisfaction goal

Basics

  • Reports to Founder/CEO (Kaden)
  • 0–2 years client-facing preferred
  • Bachelor’s preferred, not required

Realistic job preview

Let’s be real about it

This role involves a lot of outreach, plenty of “no”s, and pay that’s partly commission. You’ll need to relocate to Lehi—on-site, no relocation assistance. We say this up front so you can decide if the mentorship and mission are worth the grind.

  • Cold outreach is the job

    Most days start with prospecting faculty who haven’t asked to hear from you yet.

  • Rejection is normal

    “No” is data, not a personal verdict. Coachability under rejection matters here.

  • Commission is part of pay

    Base is $50–60K. The rest depends on pipeline you build and deals you help close.

  • Lehi, on-site

    This is an in-office role in Lehi, UT. Relocation is required; we do not offer relocation assistance.

Hiring process

What to expect when you apply

A seven-step path designed to find coachable sellers who can demo with clarity. Plan on roughly 10–11 weeks from posting to offer (e.g., February post → May start → summer ramp → August push).

  1. 1

    Resume screen

    We look for signal of grit, communication, and interest in education or sales.

  2. 2

    15-min phone screen

    A short conversation on fit, relocation readiness, and motivation.

  3. 3

    Written cold-email exercise

    Show how you’d open a door with a professor who doesn’t know Prova yet.

  4. 4

    Mock product demo + coaching

    Run a demo, then take feedback live. (Can be same-day / video for out-of-state.)

  5. 5

    Structured interview

    Deeper conversation on judgment, ownership, and how you learn. Score ≥3/5 to advance.

  6. 6

    References + background check

    We confirm the story with people who’ve seen you work.

  7. 7

    Offer

    If it’s a mutual fit, we make it official and plan your May start / summer ramp.

Final evaluation weights: mock demo 35% · structured interview 30% · written exercise 20% · references 15%. Ties favor coachability and improvement across rounds.
